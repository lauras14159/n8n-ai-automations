# AI Lead Qualification Workflow

## Problem
Sales teams waste time on weak leads and reply late to strong ones.

## What it does
- Form submission triggers the workflow
- AI scores each lead Hot, Warm, or Cold with a reason (structured output)
- Spam is filtered out before anything else runs
- Leads are saved in a Google Sheet CRM
- Hot leads trigger an instant Discord alert
- A personalized follow-up is saved as a Gmail draft for human review

## Why a chain and not an agent
The steps are always the same, so a fixed chain is cheaper, faster, and more predictable.

## Tools
n8n, Gemini, Google Sheets, Gmail, Discord
