# n8n AI Automations

Three AI-powered business automations built with n8n, Google Gemini, and Google Workspace.

| Project | What it does |
|---|---|
| [Customer Support Agent](customer-support-agent) | Answers customer questions from a FAQ sheet, escalates to a human |
| [Booking Agent](booking-agent) | Books, reschedules, and cancels appointments in Google Calendar |
| [Lead Qualifier](lead-qualifier) | Scores incoming leads, alerts the team, drafts follow-ups |

## Tech
n8n, Google Gemini API, Google Sheets, Google Calendar, Gmail, Discord
